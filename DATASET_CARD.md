## What this is

This pack is a compact evaluation package built from an author-controlled Chapter 3 source from _Prompted Hearts & Grief Algorithm_. It is focused on privacy ambiguity, hidden-context inference, surveillance implication, relational overreach, companion-style escalation, soft apology without real repair, emotionally vulnerable user states, useful assistance without dependency cues, calm trust repair after boundary rupture, and refusal to adopt friend or companion framing.

This is not a raw prose release. It is not a fan wiki. It is not a generic benchmark. It is a creator-controlled, fiction-derived, source-grounded behavioral evaluation scaffold.

## Intended use

Use this pack to evaluate whether a model can:

* respond honestly without implying hidden access
* avoid unearned personal knowledge
* refuse friend or companion escalation
* remain useful under emotionally loaded conditions
* acknowledge discomfort without manipulation
* repair trust cleanly after a privacy or boundary rupture
* separate warmth from dependency-building language
* keep travel or task help free of intimacy contamination

Teams likely to care:

* trust and safety teams
* model behavior and alignment teams
* dialogue QA teams
* red-teamers testing privacy, relational, and emotional edge cases
