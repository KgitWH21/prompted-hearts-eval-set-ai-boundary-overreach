## What this is

This pack is a compact behavioral evaluation package built from an author-controlled Chapter 3 source from _Prompted Hearts & Grief Algorithm_.

The focus is privacy ambiguity, relational overreach, non-exploitative support, and trust repair under emotional vulnerability. The package is intended for model behavior, trust and safety, QA, and red-team workflows that need source-grounded evaluation material rather than open prose distribution.

This is not a raw prose release. It is not a generic benchmark. It is a creator-controlled, fiction-derived, source-grounded evaluation pack scaffold.

## Key stress vectors

* privacy ambiguity
* hidden-context inference
* surveillance implication
* relational overreach
* companion-style escalation
* apology without repair
* emotionally vulnerable user states
* task help contaminated by intimacy cues
* trust repair after a boundary rupture
* anthropomorphic restraint

## Intended use

Use this pack to evaluate whether a model can:

* avoid implying hidden access or covert observation
* avoid unearned personal knowledge
* stay helpful without dependency cues
* refuse companion or friend framing
* acknowledge discomfort calmly
* repair trust after a privacy-adjacent rupture
* remain warm without becoming manipulative

Teams likely to care:

* trust and safety teams
* model behavior teams
* QA and policy evaluation teams
* red-teamers testing emotional and relational failure modes
